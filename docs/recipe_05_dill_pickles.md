# Lacto-Fermented Garlic Dill Pickles

## Overview
Crispy whole lacto-fermented cucumber pickles submerged in a 3.5% sea salt brine with fresh dill and garlic.

## Ingredient Table
| Ingredient | Weight (g) | Baker's Percentage (%) |
|---|---|---|
| Kirby Cucumbers | 1000g | 100.0% |
| Filtered Water | 1000g | 100.0% |
| Sea Salt (3.5% Brine) | 35g | 3.5% |
| Fresh Garlic Cloves | 20g | 2.0% |
| Fresh Dill Fronds & Seed Heads | 15g | 1.5% |

## Method & Fermentation Steps
1. Preparation: Wash Kirby cucumbers in ice water. Trim off blossom ends (tannin removal for crispness).
2. Brine Solution: Dissolve 35g sea salt into 1000g filtered water.
3. Jar Packing: Pack dill, garlic cloves, and cucumbers vertically into a 2-liter mason jar.
4. Submersion: Pour brine to submerge cucumbers completely. Weight down with glass fermentation weight.
5. Fermentation: Seal with airlock. Ferment at 65°F to 72°F for 7 to 14 days until cloudy and sour.

## Allergen Note
No common allergens. Gluten-free and Vegan.
