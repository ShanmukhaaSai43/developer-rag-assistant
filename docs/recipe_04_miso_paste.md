# Traditional Red Rice Miso

## Overview
Umami-rich Japanese fermented soybean and rice koji paste aged for 6 months.

## Ingredient Table
| Ingredient | Weight (g) | Baker's Percentage (%) |
|---|---|---|
| Dried Soybeans | 500g | 100.0% |
| Rice Koji (Aspergillus oryzae) | 500g | 100.0% |
| Sea Salt | 210g | 42.0% |
| Reserved Soybean Cooking Water | 150g | 30.0% |

## Method & Fermentation Steps
1. Soak & Cook: Soak dried soybeans in water for 18 hours. Pressure cook for 45 minutes until soft enough to crush between thumb and pinky finger. Reserve cooking liquid.
2. Mash & Salt: Mash hot cooked soybeans thoroughly. Cool to 95°F (35°C). Mix sea salt with rice koji.
3. Combine: Thoroughly hand-mix mashed soybeans, salted koji, and reserved cooking liquid into tight balls.
4. Packing & Aging: Pack tightly into crock, eliminating all air pockets. Sprinkle salt barrier on top, weight down with 2kg stone. Ferment for 6 months at room temperature.

## Allergen Note
Contains Soy. Gluten-free.
