# Primary Kombucha Ferment

## Overview
A refreshing probiotic fermented black tea beverage made with a active SCOBY culture.

## Ingredient Table
| Ingredient | Weight (g) | Baker's Percentage (%) |
|---|---|---|
| Filtered Water | 3000g | 100.0% |
| Organic Cane Sugar | 210g | 7.0% |
| Loose Black Tea | 15g | 0.5% |
| Unpasteurized Starter Tea | 300g | 10.0% |
| Active SCOBY Culture | 150g | 5.0% |

## Method & Fermentation Steps
1. Sweet Tea Base: Steep loose black tea in 1000g boiling water for 10 minutes. Strain tea leaves, stir in organic cane sugar until dissolved.
2. Dilution & Cooling: Add remaining 2000g cool filtered water until liquid drops below 75°F (24°C).
3. Inoculation: Pour sweet tea into fermentation vessel, add unpasteurized starter tea, and float SCOBY on top.
4. Primary Fermentation: Cover vessel with breathable cloth. Ferment in dark cabinet at 72°F to 78°F for 7 to 10 days. Target pH: 2.8 - 3.2.

## Allergen Note
No common allergens. Caffeine present.
