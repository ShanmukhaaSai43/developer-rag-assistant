# Traditional Baechu Kimchi

## Overview
Traditional Korean fermented Napa cabbage kimchi with a rich gochugaru chili paste and anchovy fish sauce.

## Ingredient Table
| Ingredient | Weight (g) | Baker's Percentage (%) |
|---|---|---|
| Napa Cabbage | 2000g | 100.0% |
| Coarse Sea Salt | 100g | 5.0% |
| Gochugaru (Chili Flakes) | 80g | 4.0% |
| Fish Sauce (Anchovy) | 40g | 2.0% |
| Minced Garlic | 40g | 2.0% |
| Minced Ginger | 20g | 1.0% |

## Method & Fermentation Steps
1. Salting: Quarter Napa cabbage, rub coarse sea salt between leaves, and soak in brine for 6 hours until pliable. Rinse thoroughly 3 times to remove excess salt.
2. Paste Preparation: Blend gochugaru, fish sauce, minced garlic, and minced ginger into a smooth paste.
3. Coat: Pack chili paste evenly between every cabbage leaf.
4. Fermentation: Pack tightly into glass jar, press down to submerge under brine. Ferment at 68°F for 3 to 5 days until bubbly, then transfer to cold storage at 38°F.

## Allergen Note
Contains Fish (Anchovy). Gluten-free product.
