# Country Sourdough Bread

## Overview
A classic naturally leavened rustic country sourdough loaf with 75% hydration and 20% sourdough starter inoculate.

## Ingredient Table
| Ingredient | Weight (g) | Baker's Percentage (%) |
|---|---|---|
| Bread Flour | 1000g | 100.0% |
| Water | 750g | 75.0% |
| Active Sourdough Starter | 200g | 20.0% |
| Fine Sea Salt | 20g | 2.0% |

## Method & Fermentation Steps
1. Autolyse: Mix flour and 700g water; rest for 45 minutes at room temperature (78°F).
2. Inoculation & Salt: Add starter and remaining 50g water with fine sea salt. Mix thoroughly.
3. Bulk Fermentation: Perform 4 sets of stretch-and-folds spaced 30 minutes apart. Total bulk time: 4.5 hours.
4. Shaping: Preshape, bench rest 20 minutes, then final shape into a round banneton.
5. Cold Proof: Retard in refrigerator at 38°F for 14 hours.
6. Baking: Preheat Dutch oven to 450°F (230°C). Bake covered 20 mins, uncovered 22 mins.

## Allergen Note
Contains Wheat (Gluten). Manufactured in a facility that handles nuts and sesame.
