# Lacto-Fermented Blueberries

## Overview
Modernist vacuum-sealed lacto-fermented wild blueberries with 2% non-iodized sea salt for savory/acidic fruit topping.

## Ingredient Table
| Ingredient | Weight (g) | Baker's Percentage (%) |
|---|---|---|
| Fresh Blueberries | 500g | 100.0% |
| Non-Iodized Fine Sea Salt | 10g | 2.0% |

## Method & Fermentation Steps
1. Weigh & Toss: Gently wash and dry blueberries. Toss 500g berries thoroughly with 10g fine sea salt in a bowl.
2. Vacuum Seal: Transfer salted berries into a vacuum pouch in a single layer. Seal at maximum vacuum pressure.
3. Dry Fermentation: Store pouch flat at 80°F (27°C) for 4 to 6 days until pouch expands like a pillow with CO2 gas.
4. Storage: Snip pouch open, strain generated savory blueberry juice, and refrigerate berries at 38°F.

## Allergen Note
No common allergens. Gluten-free and Vegan.
