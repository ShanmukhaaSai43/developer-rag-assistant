# Lacto-Fermented Blueberries (Noma Style)

## Overview
A modernist culinary technique using 2.0% salt by weight in a vacuum-sealed bag to create floral, effervescent, and savory-sweet lacto-fermented berries.

## Ingredient Table
| Ingredient | Weight (g) | Baker's Percentage (%) |
|---|---|---|
| Fresh Organic Blueberries | 500g | 100.0% |
| Non-Iodized Fine Sea Salt | 10g | 2.0% |

## Method & Fermentation Steps
1. Weighing: Rinse and thoroughly dry blueberries. Weigh precisely on a digital scale (500g).
2. Salting: Calculate exactly 2.0% salt (10g). Toss blueberries with salt in a stainless steel bowl until berries are evenly frosted with salt crystals.
3. Vacuum Sealing: Transfer salted berries to a food-grade vacuum bag. Seal on full vacuum pressure without crushing berries.
4. Fermentation Window: Place bag flat in a dark incubator or room temperature spot (82°F / 28°C).
5. Monitoring: Over 4 to 5 days, the bag will inflate like a balloon from carbon dioxide production.
6. Finishing: Pierce bag, drain and reserve lacto-blueberry juice, store berries in an airtight jar refrigerated at 38°F.

## Allergen Note
Naturally vegan, dairy-free, nut-free, and gluten-free. No added preservatives.
