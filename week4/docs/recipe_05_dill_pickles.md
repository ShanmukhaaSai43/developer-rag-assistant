# Naturally Fermented Garlic Dill Pickles

## Overview
Crisp full-sour deli pickles fermented in a 3.5% sea salt brine with fresh dill heads, mustard seed, and oak leaves for natural tannin crunch.

## Ingredient Table
| Ingredient | Weight (g) | Baker's Percentage (%) |
|---|---|---|
| Kirby Pickling Cucumbers | 1000g | 100.0% |
| Filtered Water | 1000g | 100.0% |
| Non-Iodized Pickling Salt | 35g | 3.5% |
| Fresh Flowering Dill Heads | 20g | 2.0% |
| Garlic Cloves (Crushed) | 25g | 2.5% |
| Yellow Mustard Seeds | 5g | 0.5% |
| Black Peppercorns | 4g | 0.4% |
| Fresh Tannin Leaves (Oak/Grape) | 2 leaves | N/A |

## Method & Fermentation Steps
1. Cucumber Prep: Trim 1/16th inch blossom end off cucumbers (contains softening enzymes). Soak in ice water for 30 minutes.
2. Brine Creation: Whisk 35g pickling salt into 1000g cold filtered water until completely dissolved (3.5% brine).
3. Jar Packing: Layer garlic, dill heads, mustard seeds, peppercorns, and tannin leaves at the bottom of a wide-mouth jar. Pack cucumbers vertically as tightly as possible.
4. Submersion: Pour brine over cucumbers to submerge completely. Place a glass fermentation weight on top.
5. Fermentation Time: Ferment at 68°F–72°F (20°C–22°C) for 7 days (half-sour) or 14 to 21 days (full-sour).

## Allergen Note
Naturally gluten-free, nut-free, and vegan. Contains Mustard (Mustard Seeds).
