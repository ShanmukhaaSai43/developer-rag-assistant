# Traditional Baechu Kimchi

## Overview
A traditional Korean fermented cabbage dish salted with coarse salt, seasoned with gochugaru, garlic, ginger, and fermented at room temperature.

## Ingredient Table
| Ingredient | Weight (g) | Baker's Percentage (%) |
|---|---|---|
| Napa Cabbage | 2000g | 100.0% |
| Coarse Korean Sea Salt | 150g | 7.5% |
| Korean Chili Flakes (Gochugaru) | 80g | 4.0% |
| Garlic Cloves | 50g | 2.5% |
| Fresh Ginger | 20g | 1.0% |
| Fish Sauce (Anchovy) | 40g | 2.0% |
| Fermented Salted Shrimp (Saeujeot) | 25g | 1.25% |

## Method & Fermentation Steps
1. Salting: Quarter Napa cabbage, rub coarse sea salt between leaves, and soak in brine for 6 hours.
2. Rinsing: Rinse thoroughly 3 times with cold water to remove excess surface salt and drain for 2 hours.
3. Paste Preparation: Blend garlic, ginger, fish sauce, salted shrimp, and gochugaru into a thick seasoning paste.
4. Rubbing: Generously coat each leaf with seasoning paste.
5. Fermentation: Pack tightly into an airtight onggi or glass jar. Ferment at 65°F (18°C) for 3 days, then store at 36°F in refrigerator.

## Allergen Note
Contains Fish (Anchovy) and Crustacean Shellfish (Shrimp). Gluten-free.
