# Primary Kombucha Fermentation

## Overview
A tangy, effervescent fermented tea created via a symbiotic culture of bacteria and yeast (SCOBY) converting sucrose into organic acids.

## Ingredient Table
| Ingredient | Weight (g) | Baker's Percentage (%) |
|---|---|---|
| Filtered Spring Water | 3500g | 100.0% |
| Organic Cane Sugar | 250g | 7.14% |
| Black Tea Leaves (Assam) | 25g | 0.71% |
| Active Kombucha Starter Liquid | 350g | 10.0% |
| Mature SCOBY Pellicle | 150g | 4.28% |

## Method & Fermentation Steps
1. Tea Brew: Steep tea in 1000g boiling water for 15 minutes. Strain tea leaves.
2. Sugar Dissolution: Dissolve organic cane sugar in hot tea concentrate.
3. Dilution: Add remaining 2500g cold filtered water to bring brew temperature down below 80°F (27°C).
4. Inoculation: Pour active starter liquid and place SCOBY on top. Cover vessel with breathable cotton cloth and rubber band.
5. Primary Fermentation: Ferment undisturbed in darkness at 75°F–80°F (24°C–27°C) for 7 to 10 days until pH reaches 2.8 to 3.2.

## Allergen Note
Naturally gluten-free and vegan. Contains trace caffeine and residual ethanol (<0.5% ABV).
