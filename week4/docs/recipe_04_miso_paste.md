# Traditional Red Miso Paste (Akamiso)

## Overview
A rich, deeply umami fermented soybean paste made from cooked soybeans, rice koji (*Aspergillus oryzae*), and sea salt aged for 6 to 12 months.

## Ingredient Table
| Ingredient | Weight (g) | Baker's Percentage (%) |
|---|---|---|
| Dried Soybeans | 500g | 100.0% |
| Rice Koji (Inoculated) | 500g | 100.0% |
| Non-Iodized Fine Sea Salt | 230g | 46.0% |
| Reserved Soybean Cooking Liquid | 150g | 30.0% |

## Method & Fermentation Steps
1. Soak & Boil: Soak dried soybeans for 18 hours. Simmer for 4 hours until beans can be crushed between thumb and pinky.
2. Mashing: Drain and reserve 150g liquid. Mash hot cooked soybeans into a smooth paste.
3. Mixing: Cool beans to 100°F (38°C). Mix with rice koji, sea salt, and reserved liquid until homogenous.
4. Packing: Form paste into balls and throw firmly into a ceramic crock to eliminate air pockets.
5. Salting Surface: Sprinkle 20g salt over the top surface, apply plastic wrap directly, and weight down with 2kg weights.
6. Aging: Ferment at room temperature in a dark pantry for 6 to 12 months.

## Allergen Note
Contains Soy. Gluten-free if rice koji is cultivated on 100% white rice.
